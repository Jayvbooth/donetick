# Domain glossary

## Circle

The shared Donetick household or support workspace whose members can collaborate on shared items.

## Member

A person with a separate username and password who belongs to a Circle.

## Supporter

A Member who helps with shared responsibilities or responds to an explicit help request. Supporter does not mean unrestricted access to another Member's private items.

## Task

A concrete action that can be completed once. In the existing code, a Task is represented by a Chore with one-time scheduling.

## Routine

A Task that repeats according to a schedule. In the existing code, a Routine is represented by a recurring Chore.

## Goal

A desired outcome that gives related Tasks and Milestones a shared purpose.

## Milestone

A meaningful checkpoint within a Goal. A Milestone may have a target date and point reward.

## Calendar Item

A non-task fact tied to time, such as an appointment, event, birthday, work shift, or family event.

## Bill

A one-time or recurring money obligation with an amount, due date, responsibility, and payment state.

## Reminder

A scheduled prompt. A Reminder may belong to a Task, Bill, Calendar Item, Goal Milestone, or stand alone.

## Daily Reset

The end-of-day flow that resolves unfinished Tasks, reviews tomorrow, and closes the day without requiring perfection.

## Points

The existing Donetick household score balance.

## XP

The user-facing presentation of Points when showing progress, levels, completion rewards, and recovery.

## Scoring Policy

The small set of rules that converts a Task result into a point change based on base value, timing mode, lateness, recovery, and streak eligibility.

## Timing Mode

How time affects a Task's score: untimed, today, deadline, or window.

## Blocker

A Member-selected reason a Task cannot currently proceed, such as missing information, unclear steps, overwhelm, or needing help.

## AI Inbox

A typed or spoken raw capture that produces editable proposals for Tasks, subtasks, Calendar Items, Bills, Reminders, Goals, and Milestones. Proposals do not become real records until a Member approves them.

## Shared Item

An item visible to the Circle according to existing visibility rules.

## Private Item

An item visible only to its creator and permitted assignees according to existing Donetick privacy behavior.
